Questions

Quick answers before you order.

How do I order?

Tap Add to order on the items you want, open Your order and send it on WhatsApp. We reply to confirm everything.

Can I order many pieces at once?

Yes. Put the quantities in your order and we confirm the price for larger numbers when we reply.

Do you deliver and install?

Yes, we offer delivery and installation. Tell us your location on WhatsApp and we confirm the cost and timing.

How do I pay?

Pay with M-Pesa to 0700 357 702, then send your order on WhatsApp with the M-Pesa confirmation code. We confirm once we see the payment.

Can I ask for a different colour or finish?

Message us the item and what you have in mind, and we tell you what is possible.
